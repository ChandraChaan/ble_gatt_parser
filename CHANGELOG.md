## 0.1.0

- First release.
- Parses Heart Rate, Body Sensor Location, Blood Pressure, Intermediate Cuff Pressure, Temperature, Intermediate Temperature, Weight, Glucose, PLX Continuous and Spot-check, Cycling Speed and Cadence, Cycling Power, RSC, Battery Level, Device Name and Device Information strings.
- Decodes IEEE 11073 SFLOAT and FLOAT, including special values.
- Adds cadence and speed helpers that handle counter wrap-around.
